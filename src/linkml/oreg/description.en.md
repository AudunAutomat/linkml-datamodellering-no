The OREG domain (Offentlege register — public registers) contains early, exploratory domain models for Norwegian registers: `enhetsregisteret-bvrinnfelles` (the Central Coordinating Register for Legal Entities, Enhetsregisteret) and `register-over-aksjeeiere` (the Register of Shareholders, Aksjeeigarregisteret).

The models are proof-of-concept and business object models — they are not as mature as the NGR models, but are used to explore LinkML-based register modeling.

**Typical user:** Developers who want to experiment with LinkML modeling of register data from the Brønnøysund Register Centre.
