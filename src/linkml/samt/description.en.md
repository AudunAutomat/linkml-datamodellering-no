The SAMT domain (Samarbeid om Modernisering av Tenesteforvalting — collaboration on modernizing service management) contains cross-cutting interaction models for the municipal sector. `samt-bu` covers services related to children and young people, including school information, student data and organizational structure for collaborating municipalities.

**Typical user:** Municipal and public bodies collaborating on modernizing services for children and young people, and developers implementing cross-cutting integrations.
