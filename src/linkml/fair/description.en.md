The FAIR domain contains one metadata model, `fair-metadata`, which complements the Norwegian application profiles (DCAT-AP-NO, SKOS-AP-NO and more) with additional properties for the [FAIR principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable).

Like the AP-NO profiles, the model is intended to be imported into domain models — it has no `tree_root` class of its own.

**Typical user:** Research institutions and public bodies that are to publish FAIR-compliant datasets, and modelers who want to extend their own domain models with FAIR metadata.
